# 广东省东莞实验中学2022-2023学年高一上学期期中考试英语试题

## 语法填空

### 原卷第 7 题

Global warming is greatly having    effect on hundreds of plant and animal species around the world. (用适当的词填空)

**答案**：an

**解析**：考查冠词。句意：全球变暖对世界各地数以百计的动植物物种产生了巨大的影响。have an effect on为固定短语，表示“对……产生影响”,effect的发音是元音音素开头，故填an。

### 原卷第 8 题

Our teachers request that attention    (focus) on the lessons in class. (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：should be focused |be focused

**解析**：考查虚拟语气和被动语态。句意：我们的老师要求在课堂上把注意力集中在课文上。分析句子结构可知，request意为“要求”，后面的宾语从句中使用虚拟语气，谓语动词使用（should）do，attention和focus为被动关系，需用被动语态。故填（should）be focused。

### 原卷第 9 题

The exam    (come) in the next month, so I am a little stressed. (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：is coming

**解析**：考查动词时态。句意：下个月就要考试了，所以我有点紧张。根据时间状语in the next month可知，此处为一般将来时，come是表示位置移动的动词，用现在进行时表示将来，主语为the exam，be动词用is。故填is coming。

### 原卷第 10 题

Practising Chinese kung fu can not only build up one’s   (strong),but also develop one’s character. (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：strength

**解析**：考查名词。句意：练习中国功夫不仅可以增强一个人的力量，而且还可以培养一个人的性格。分析句子，设空处使用strong的名词strength作宾语，意为“力量”，不可数，故填strength。

### 原卷第 11 题

The new teaching building is big and well-equipped,    (compare) with the old one. (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：compared

**解析**：考查非谓语动词。句意：与旧教学楼相比，新教学楼又大设备又齐全。分析句子结构可知，已有谓语动词is，空处需填非谓语动词作状语，The new teaching building和compare为逻辑动宾关系，需用过去分词作状语。故填compared。

### 原卷第 12 题

The local government and volunteers would supply survivors    shelters. (用适当的词填空)

**答案**：with

**解析**：考查介词。句意：当地政府和志愿者将为幸存者提供庇护所。supply sb. with sth.为固定短语，意为“向某人提供某物”。故填with。

### 原卷第 13 题

This was an amazing and rewarding activity of first aid    made a deep impression on every student in our grade. (用适当的词填空)

**答案**：which

**解析**：考查定语从句。句意：这是一次精彩而有益的急救活动，给我们年级的每个学生都留下了深刻的印象。空处引导定语从句，先行词activity，指物，在定语从句中作主语，需用关系代词which引导。故填which。

### 原卷第 14 题

I’ve been learning English more than for 10 years, and I speak it    (fluent). (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：fluently

**解析**：考查副词。句意：我学英语已经有10多年了，并且我说得很流利。修饰动词speak，需用副词fluently作状语。故填fluently。

### 原卷第 15 题

Sometimes the little boy pretended    (read) a book on the sofa to make his parents praise him. (所给词的适当形式填空)

**答案**：to read

**解析**：考查非谓语动词。句意：有时小男孩假装在沙发上看书，让他的父母称赞他。pretend to do sth.为固定搭配，表示“假装做某事”，空处需填动词不定式to read作宾语。故填to read。

### 原卷第 16 题

Emergency and foreign aid was organized, but damaged roads would make    difficult to deliver goods and supplies. (用适当的词填空)

**答案**：it

**解析**：考查it作形式宾语。句意：已经组织了紧急援助和外国援助，但道路受损将使货物和供应品难以运送。make it+形容词+to do...为固定句型，表示“使做某事变得……”，it是形式宾语，真正的宾语是动词不定式。故填it。

## 完成句子

### 原卷第 17 题

As is known to all, the history of glass    (追溯到) 3500 BC. (根据汉语提示完成句子)

**答案**：dates back to

**解析**：考查固定短语和动词时态。句意：众所周知，玻璃的历史可以追溯到公元前3500年。根据汉语提示“追溯到”可知，此处为动词短语date back to，此处陈述客观事实，使用一般现在时，主语为the history of glass，谓语动词使用第三人称单数形式。故填dates back to。

### 原卷第 18 题

Let’s be good friends,    (反义疑问句)?

**答案**：shall we

**解析**：考查反意疑问句。句意：让我们作好朋友，好吗？Let’s开头的祈使句的反义疑问句，应用shall we。故填shall we。

### 原卷第 19 题

Some students addict themselves to online computer games. (同义句转换)

Some students    online computer games.

**答案**：are addicted to

**解析**：考查固定短语。句意：一些学生沉迷于网络电脑游戏。需要替换的部分是addict themselves to，固定短语be addicted to，表示“沉迷于”，和addict oneself to意思相同，根据原句的addict可知，此处为一般现在时，主语为复数名词，be动词用are。故填are addicted to。

### 原卷第 20 题

The speaker stood in front of the students of Grade one. (句式转换)

In front of the students of Grade one   .

**答案**：stood the speaker

**解析**：考查倒装句。句意：演讲者站在一年级学生面前。表示方位的介词短语in front of the students of Grade one置于句首，句子使用完全倒装，将谓语stood放在主语the speaker前面。故填stood the speaker。

### 原卷第 21 题

It seems that my roommate has much confidence for the coming final exam. (同义句转换)

My roommate    much confidence for the coming final exam.

**答案**：seems to have

**解析**：考查固定搭配。句意：似乎我的室友对即将到来的期末考试很有信心。将It seems that...转化为固定短语seem to do sth.，表示“似乎做某事”，主语为my roommate，谓语动词seem使用第三人称单数形式，have位于动词不定式符号to后面，使用动词原形。故填seems to have。

### 原卷第 22 题

Most of people recognized Arthur Miller to be one of the greatest dramatists of the 20th century. (同义句转换)

Arthur Miller    one of the greatest dramatists of the 20th century.

**答案**：was recognized as

**解析**：考查固定短语和被动语态。句意：大多数人都认为阿瑟·米勒是20世纪最伟大的剧作家之一。recognize sb. to be和recognize sb. as意思相同，表示“把……视为”，主语Arthur Miller和regard为被动关系，需用被动语态，根据原句中的recognized可知，此处使用一般过去时，主语为单数名词，谓语动词使用第三人称单数形式。故填was recognized as。

### 原卷第 23 题

The student’s calligraphy work was shown on the blackboard. I know the student. (用定语从句合并)

**答案**：I know the student whose calligraphy work was shown on the blackboard.

**解析**：考查定语从句。句意：学生的书法作品展示在黑板上。我认识这个学生。将I know the student作为主句，The student’s calligraphy work was shown on the blackboard作为定语从句，先行词是the student，在定语从句中作定语修饰名词calligraphy，需用关系代词whose引导。故填I know the student whose calligraphy work was shown on the blackboard.

### 原卷第 24 题

She didn’t come back home until I finished this letter. (强调划线部分)

**答案**：It was not until I finished his letter that she came back home.

**解析**：考查强调句。句意：直到我写完这封信她才回家。对时间状语从句until I finished this letter进行强调，应用固定句型It was not until+ 被强调部分+that+句子其他成分，句子的时态为一般过去时。故填It was not until I finished his letter that she came back home.

## 翻译

### 原卷第 25 题

Jane 更喜欢自己解决问题而不是向他人寻求帮助。(汉译英)

**答案**：Jane would rather deal with the problem by herself than ask others for help. Jane prefers to deal with the problem by herself rather than ask others for help. Jane prefers dealing with the problem by herself to asking others for help.

**解析**：考查固定搭配和动词时态。表示“更喜欢……而不是……”应用固定句型would rather do...than do.../prefer to do...rather than do.../prefer doing...to doing...；表示“解决”应用deal with，在前两个句型中使用动词原形，第三个句型中使用动名词形式；表示“问题”应用the problem；表示“靠自己”应用by herself；表示“向某人求助”应用ask sb. for help，在前两个句型中，使用动词原形，在第三个句型中使用动名词形式；表示“其他人”应用others；句子陈述客观事实，使用一般现在时，主语为Jane，谓语动词使用第三人称单数形式。故翻译成：Jane would rather deal with the problem by herself than ask others for help./Jane prefers to deal with the problem by herself rather than ask others for help./Jane prefers dealing with the problem by herself to asking others for help.。

## 书面表达

### 原卷第 26 题

假设你是李华，下周你们国际学校将进行第二十五届校运会，请你在开幕仪式上致辞。

1. 健康生活的重要性；

2. 健康生活的建议；

3. 呼吁和提出希望。

注意:   1. 词数：80 左右；

2. 可适当增加细节，以使行文连贯；

3. 开头和结尾已给出，不计入总词数。

Hello everyone,   

_________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

That’s all. Thank you!

**答案**：Hello everyone, I’m Li Hua, a student in the International Senoir High School. There will be the 25th sports meeting in my school, and I feel extremely honored to deliver a speech at the opening ceremony. As is known to all, healthy living habits can keep us fit, protect us from getting ill and help us start a new day with full energy. For the purpose, our school holds the sports meeting every year. In order to build up physical and even mental strength, it’s necessary for us to take effective action. Firstly, we’d better have a balanced diet, such as taking more fresh fruits, meat and vegetables, while less snacks. Furthermore, what matters a lot is setting aside enough time for daily exercise, which is beneficial to our health. Last but not least, having enough sleep will surely keep you energetic for a whole day. If you follow these suggestions and start a healthy lifestyle, I firmly believe that you will lead a happy life. Let’s exercise and actively take part in the school sports meeting. That’s all. Thank you!

**解析**：本篇书面表达是一篇应用文。要求考生写一篇演讲稿，在学校第二十五届校运会开幕仪式上致辞。

1.词汇积累

帮助：help→assist

健康的：fit→healthy

首先：firstly→to start with

参加：take part in→participate in

2.句式拓展

简单句变复合句

原句：I’m Li Hua, a student in the International Senoir High School.

拓展句：I’m Li Hua, who is a student in the International Senoir High School.

[高分句型1]

As is known to all, healthy living habits can keep us fit, protect us from getting ill and help us start a new day with full energy.（运用了as引导非限制性定语从句）

[高分句型2]

If you follow these suggestions and start a healthy lifestyle, I firmly believe that you will lead a happy life.（运用了if引导条件状语从句，that引导宾语从句）
