否定、相反与缺失

un-　不 / 相反 / 未发生

核心方向

un- 是最适合入门的否定前缀。它常放在形容词或分词前面，让原来的意义反过来，或表示某件事没有发生。

例词

able → unable certain → uncertain usual → unusual willing → unwilling expected → unexpected known → unknown comfortable → uncomfortable

真题例句

These days, it is not unusual for 10- to 12-year-olds to publish their own websites.

如今，10 到 12 岁的孩子发布自己的网站并不稀奇。

Several students were unable to ever stay after school to complete research.

有几个学生根本无法在放学后留下来完成研究。

dis-　反向 / 取消 / 相反

1 动作反向或取消

dis- 常和动词结合，表示动作方向被反过来，或原来的动作、状态被取消。

例词

appear → disappear agree → disagree like → dislike

2 状态、态度或条件相反

dis- 也可以和名词、形容词相关的词结合，表示状态、态度或条件与原词相反。

例词

comfort → discomfort content → discontent advantage → disadvantage courage → discouraged

discover, discovered, discovery 虽然很常见，但这里不要按 dis- 表示“不、相反”来理解。

真题例句

Two basic magic tricks are making objects seem to appear and making objects seem to disappear.

两种基本魔术是让物体看起来出现，以及让物体看起来消失。

This approach spared me the discomfort of having to over-explain.

这种做法让我免去了不得不过度解释的尴尬和不适。

in- / im- / il- / ir-

1 同一组，不同拼写外形

in- / im- / il- / ir- 都常表示“不、相反、缺少”。它们常和形容词或相关名词结合。

例词

ability → inability formal → informal security → insecurity possible → impossible patient → impatient perfect → imperfect legal → illegal relevant → irrelevant replaceable → irreplaceable

2 拼写规律：只当识别工具

im- 常出现在 p / m / b 前；il- 常出现在 l 前；ir- 常出现在 r 前；in- 是更普通的形式。

例词

impossible impatient imperfect illegal irrelevant irreplaceable inability informal insecurity

这条规律不是用来随便造词的。读到词时，先认出“这可能是一组否定前缀”，再回到句子里验证意思。

真题例句

Having a poor sense of direction, Eva found it impossible to get around in such a huge building.

由于方向感很差，伊娃发现自己不可能在这样一栋大楼里顺利走动。

The inability to engage with longer-term causes and consequences leads to some of the world's most serious problems.

无法关注更长期的原因和后果，会导致世界上一些最严重的问题。

However, because they are usually writing to family and friends, they use informal language most of the time.

然而，因为他们通常是在给家人和朋友写信息，所以大多数时候会使用非正式语言。

Pangolins are hunted illegally across Africa for their scales, which are used for traditional medicine, and their meat.

穿山甲在非洲各地被非法猎杀，人们猎杀它们是为了获取可用于传统药物的鳞片以及它们的肉。

Indeed, to a computer, meaning is irrelevant.

事实上，对于计算机来说，意义并不相关。

To be on the safe side, don't let your child bring anything irreplaceable or emotionally valuable to camp.

为了稳妥起见，不要让孩子带任何不可替代或有情感价值的东西去营地。

non-　非某类 / 不具备某特征

核心方向

non- 常和名词、形容词结合，表示“非 X”“不属于 X”“不具备 X 的特征”。它和 un- 都能表示“不”，但 non- 更像类别判断。

例词

non-profit non-fiction non-credit non-verbal non-human non-carbon

unhappy　状态相反

non-profit　类别判断

真题例句

The Pre-College Program offers non-credit courses.

大学预科项目提供非学分课程。

The DRF Writers Award invites submissions of 15,000-20,000 words of a work in progress, which may be fiction or non-fiction.

DRF 作家奖征集 15,000 到 20,000 词的未完成作品，可以是小说，也可以是非虚构作品。

Of course, if you are a non-human species, collapse is well underway.

当然，如果你是非人类物种，崩溃已经在发生。

de-　去掉 / 降低 / 减少

核心方向

看到 de- 开头的词时，可以先留意它是否表示“去掉、降低、减少”。有些词可以这样帮助理解。

例词

de-ice　remove ice

decrease decline decreased carbon → decarbonize

真题例句

Normally, towns use rock salt to de-ice streets.

通常，城镇会用岩盐给街道除冰。

Our study showed that there was a 30% decrease in sick leave for people who were in plant-rich workplaces.

我们的研究显示，在植物丰富的工作场所中，人们的病假减少了 30%。

The hardest sector to decarbonize is aviation.

最难实现脱碳的领域是航空业。

counter-　对抗 / 抵消 / 对应

核心方向

counter- 常提示“反着来、对抗、抵消”的方向。看到这类词时，要注意它不是普通的“不”。

例词

counteract　act against something

counterpart(s)　corresponding person or thing

counter- 不是简单的“不”，而是“对着来、抵消它”。

counteract 表示“对抗、抵消”；counterpart(s) 表示“对应的人或物”。两者都值得认识，但不要把 counterpart(s) 硬讲成对抗。

真题例句

Anyone who considers river time in relation to atomic time will encounter a major imbalance and may be motivated to counteract it by consuming less fuel or supporting greener policies.

任何把河流时间与原子时间联系起来思考的人，都会看到一种巨大的失衡，并可能受到推动，通过减少燃料消耗或支持更环保的政策来抵消这种失衡。

Artificially sweetened diet drinks make no difference to weight gain and should not be seen as healthier than their sugar-laden counterparts, according to a team of experts.

一组专家认为，人工甜味的无糖饮料对体重增加没有影响，也不应被看作比对应的含糖饮料更健康。

By comparing these stars with their more distant counterparts, they refined their measurement of the Hubble constant.

通过把这些恒星与更遥远的对应星体进行比较，他们改进了对哈勃常数的测量。

时间顺序

pre-　之前 / 预先

核心方向

pre- 常提示“在……之前、预先”。看到 pre-X 时，可以先把 X 当作后面的事件、阶段或状态，再判断它是不是“在 X 之前”。

例词

college → pre-college presentation → pre-presentation seeded → pre-seeded date → pre-date classical → pre-Classical

真题例句

The Pre-College Program offers non-credit courses.

大学预科项目提供非学分课程。

After harvest, users simply replace the plants with a new pre-seeded pod to get the next growth cycle started.

收获之后，用户只需把植物换成一个新的预播种容器，就能开始下一轮生长。

I joined a pre-presentation dinner with him and that smaller setting allowed me to slowly build up my courage.

我参加了他演讲前的晚餐，那个更小的场合让我慢慢建立起勇气。

post-　之后 / 后续

核心方向

post- 常提示“在某事之后、后续阶段”。看到 post-X 时，先判断它是不是发生在 X 之后。

例词

visit → post-visit lunch → post-lunch production → post-production graduate → postgraduate

真题例句

Participation in a post-visit survey is required.

参加访问后的调查是必须的。

When I decided to pursue a postgraduate degree, I wanted to develop a novel research programme and quickly establish myself as an independent scientist.

当我决定攻读研究生学位时，我想开展一个新的研究项目，并迅速让自己成为独立的科学家。

mid-　中间 / 中期

核心方向

mid- 常提示“中间、中期”。它特别容易出现在时间表达里，表示一天、月份、年代或阶段的中间。

例词

night → midnight September → mid-September 1990s → mid-1990s

真题例句

I woke up at midnight to find a swimming pool in my tent.

我半夜醒来，发现帐篷里像有一个游泳池。

In the mid-1990s, Toni Bissell taught English as a volunteer in Uzbekistan.

20 世纪 90 年代中期，Toni Bissell 在乌兹别克斯坦做志愿者教英语。

On a cool, starry night in mid-September 1870, four men relaxed before a campfire along the Firehole River in what is now northwestern Wyoming.

1870 年 9 月中旬一个凉爽、星光闪烁的夜晚，四名男子在如今怀俄明州西北部 Firehole 河边的篝火前休息。

fore-　前面 / 预先

核心方向

fore- 频率不高，但方向清楚，常提示“前面、预先”。forecast 是预先估计，forehead 是头的前部。

例词

forecast forecasts forehead

真题例句

It includes engaging in short-and long-term planning, economic forecasts, and market research.

它包括进行短期和长期规划、经济预测以及市场研究。

If TMS is applied to a specific part of your brain behind your forehead that is particularly important for decision-making, you suddenly like different kinds of art.

如果经颅磁刺激作用于你前额后方一个对决策特别重要的脑区，你会突然喜欢上不同种类的艺术。

程度、数量与大小

over-　过度 / 过量 / 超过

核心方向

over- 常提示“超过合适的量、过度、过多”。有时也可以表示“超过某个界限”。

例词

weight → overweight eat → overeating fish → overfishing tourism → overtourism estimate → overestimate

真题例句

Eating alone, especially in front of the TV or computer, often leads to mindless overeating.

独自吃饭，尤其是在电视或电脑前，常常会导致无意识地吃过量。

Today, in Australia, most children on average fall 2,000 steps short of the physical activity they need to avoid being overweight.

如今在澳大利亚，大多数儿童平均还差 2000 步的身体活动量，才能避免超重。

The best way to ease pressure on over-touristed destinations is to go somewhere else.

减轻过度旅游目的地压力的最好办法，是去别的地方。

under-　不足 / 在下 / 低于

核心方向

under- 常提示“在下面、低于、没有达到”。它可以表示位置，也可以表示数量或程度不足。

例词

water → underwater ground → underground estimate → underestimate valued → undervalued served → underserved graduate → undergraduate

真题例句

The value of history cannot be underestimated.

历史的价值不可低估。

You could still hear the operatic sounds it was making underwater.

你仍然能听到它在水下发出的歌剧般的声音。

Some people will tend to overestimate, and some to underestimate.

有些人会倾向于高估，有些人会倾向于低估。

super-　超级 / 很强 / 大型

核心方向

super- 常提示“超过普通水平、很强、很大”。它常出现在现代科技、商业或日常生活词里。

例词

smart → super-smart market → supermarket

真题例句

AI is already super-smart and will become more capable.

人工智能已经非常智能，并且会变得更有能力。

Check your local supermarket for more information.

去当地超市查看更多信息。

For the rest of the time she is a super dog, and we are all really proud of her.

在其他时候，她是一只超级狗，我们都为她感到非常骄傲。

mini-　小型 / 迷你版本

核心方向

mini- 常提示“小型、缩小版”。看到 mini-X 时，先判断它是不是某个事物的小版本。

例词

mini community library mini-blanket miniature miniatures

真题例句

The last one standing has just been turned into a “mini community library”.

最后一部还立着的公共电话，刚刚被改造成一个“迷你社区图书馆”。

She also taught me to make mini-blankets.

她还教我做迷你毯子。

I spent hours of my girlhood sitting before my dollhouse, telling made-up stories, and creating miniatures.

少女时代，我常常花好几个小时坐在玩具屋前，编故事，制作微缩模型。

micro-　微小 / 微型

核心方向

micro- 常提示“很小、微型”。它常出现在科技、环境和科学阅读中。

例词

plastic → microplastic(s) chip → micro-chip scope → microscope farm → Micro-Farm

真题例句

Microplastics have become a common source of pollution across the Earth.

微塑料已经成为遍布地球的常见污染源。

Babylon Micro-Farm (BMF) shortens it even more.

Babylon Micro-Farm 让这段距离变得更短。

Microscopes and telescopes were invented by way of artistic tinkering.

显微镜和望远镜是在工匠艺术化修补的过程中发明出来的。

multi-　多个 / 多种

核心方向

multi- 常提示“多个、多种、多方面”。它可以帮我们快速判断一个词在说“不是单一的”。

例词

language → multi-language media → multimedia tasking → multitasking skilled → multi-skilled use → multi-use layered → multi-layered

真题例句

We journalists live in a new age of storytelling, with many new multimedia tools.

我们记者生活在一个讲故事的新时代，拥有许多新的多媒体工具。

The amount of time needed to build a habit will depend on multiple factors, including the individual and the intended behaviour.

养成一个习惯所需的时间取决于多个因素，包括个人以及想要形成的行为。

A waiter's job, for example, that requires multitasking, teamwork and decision-making could be just as stimulating as any high-level office work.

例如，服务员的工作需要多任务处理、团队合作和决策能力，可能和任何高级办公室工作一样能刺激大脑。

空间、方向与超出

inter-　在……之间 / 相互

核心方向

inter- 常提示“在两者或多者之间、相互之间”。它可以用于国家之间、人和人之间、道路交叉处，也可以用于互动。

例词

nation → international person → interpersonal act → interact action → interaction section → intersection play → interplay

真题例句

The chief complaint about online courses is that they lack human interaction.

人们对在线课程的主要抱怨是它们缺少人与人之间的互动。

You should possess strong interpersonal and communication skills.

你应当具备较强的人际交往和沟通能力。

规则 5：重读闭音节，双写尾字母 +ed（共 76 词，全列）

判断口令：最后一个音节重读，并且是"一个元音字母 + 一个辅音字母"收尾，才双写；input、output、programme 这类复合词跟着基词（put、gramme）走，同样双写。

input、output 过去式另可写 inputted / outputted（原形见三态表）。

不双写的三种情况（对照示例，非全列）：

- 尾音节不重读：open, listen, visit, happen, offer, suffer, develop, benefit, consider, remember, deliver, discover, enter, answer, order, wonder, limit, orbit, prohibit, interpret, credit

- 两个元音字母：eat, clean, wait, need, seem, rain, boil, join, appear, fear, fail, repeat, dream, heat

- x、w 结尾：fix, mix, relax, tax, box, allow, borrow, follow, flow, show, snow, review, interview

规则 6：英式 -l 结尾双写（共 17 词，全列）

规则 1：一般 +er / +est（兜底，不列词）

tall→taller→tallest、fast→faster→fastest……

规则 2：e 结尾 +r / +st（兜底，不列词）

nice→nicer→nicest、large→larger→largest、wide→wider→widest……

规则 3：辅音 +y → ier / iest（表内 44 词，全列）

angry busy cloudy crazy dirty dizzy dry early easy empty fancy friendly funny greedy guilty happy healthy heavy hungry lazy likely lively lonely lovely lucky merry noisy pretty rainy ready salty silly sleepy snowy sorry spicy steady sunny thirsty tidy tiny ugly windy worthy

凡"名词+y"派生的形容词同走 ier：mess→messy→messier、wealth→wealthy→wealthier、storm→stormy→stormier……

特例三个：shy→shyer（不改 y）、dry→drier/dryer 两可、grey→greyer（元音+y 直接加）。

三个音节及以上的 -y 词用 more：necessary, ordinary, primary, secondary, temporary, voluntary, elderly, literary, military, contradictory, contemporary, extraordinary。

规则 4：重读闭音节双写 +er / +est（共 12 词，全列）

big fat fit flat glad hot mad red sad slim thin wet

fun 口语里有 funner，考试写 more fun 更稳。

规则 5：双音节也能用 -er / -est（共 16 词，全列）

bitter clever common cruel gentle handsome humble narrow noble pleasant polite quiet shallow simple stupid yellow

这些用 more 也不算错，但课本和考试默认 -er/-est。cruel→crueller 英式双写 l。

eager 不在此列：写 more eager（eagerer 不规范）。

规则 6：只能 more / most 的双音节词（按后缀族全列）

判定顺序　先看是不是规则 3/4/5 的词（y 尾、重读闭音节、双音节白名单）；都不是，就用 more/most。

其他双音节散词　（32 词，全列）

able afraid alert alive aware awake awkward certain complete complex correct direct distinct diverse honest intense mature modest open perfect private proper rapid real remote rigid secure severe solid sudden valid vivid

mature/secure/remote/severe 写 -er 形式也不算硬错，但 more 更常见，按 more 记。

unique、perfect、complete 这类"到顶"的词，严格说不分程度；口语可见 more unique，考试不比。

senior、junior、superior、prior 本身已是比较级意味，后接 to 不接 than；major、minor、inner、upper、former、lower、elder 也没有常规比较级。

不能叠的规则

比较级前面不能再加 more　more taller → taller；more better → better（不规则见附注）。

Things exist or are produced by the dozen, thousand, or million 表示成打、成千、成百万地存在或生产出来。

Gillian, who wrote letters by the dozen every week, spent a fortune on stamps.

吉莉恩每周写很多信，买邮票花了一大笔钱。

It is a book which sells by the million.

这是一本销量以百万计的书。

21 逐步发生 / 一个接一个

By 用在相同的名词之间，表示某事逐渐发生或一个接一个地发生。

Ultimately, success came little by little.

最终，成功是一点一点到来的。

In the process of helping others, I could also push myself forward, bit by bit.

在帮助他人的过程中，我也能一点一点推动自己前进。

Beijing is also a place with 21st-century architectural wonders standing side by side with historical buildings of the past.

北京也是一个拥有 21 世纪建筑奇观与历史建筑并立的地方。

高频介词 of

of　22 个单项用法

1 数量 / 集合

Of 指数量或群体的名词之后，表示有关的物质或事物。

A flood had hit the area earlier that year and the damage it caused had driven away a number of birds.

那年早些时候，一场洪水袭击了这个地区，它造成的破坏赶走了许多鸟。

Such a complex but ultimately enriching set of encounters helps individuals know their neighbours and neighbourhood better.

这样一组复杂但最终令人受益的相遇，能帮助个人更好地了解邻居和社区。

2 部分 / 成员 / 位置

Of 表示事物整体与某一部分、成员或位置之间的关系。

That includes cups, bottles, and bags, most of which are only good for one use.

这包括杯子、瓶子和袋子，其中大多数只能使用一次。

At the heart of her vision is the idea that urban life should be an energetic and rich affair.

她愿景的核心，是城市生活应该充满活力、丰富多彩这一想法。

3 月份中的日期

Of 也可用来交代日期，即某天在某个月份。

The meeting was held on the 17th of June.

会议在 6 月 17 日举行。

4 种类 / 类别

Of 用在 kind、sort、type 等名词后，表示所谈论的人或事物的种类或类别。

If TMS is applied to a specific part of your brain, you suddenly like different kinds of art.

如果把经颅磁刺激施加到大脑的某个特定部位，你会突然喜欢上不同种类的艺术。

She knew they had problems, and they needed all kinds of help.

她知道他们有困难，也需要各种各样的帮助。

5 形式 / 版本 / 副本

Of 用在 version、form、copy 等名词后，说明所谈论的基本事物。

Certain forms of AI are indeed becoming ubiquitous.

某些形式的人工智能确实正在变得无处不在。

Adair sent me a copy of this letter.

阿代尔寄给我这封信的复印件。

6 所属 / 性质来源

Of 表示某事物或某种特性所属的人或东西。

We were staying at a research camp outside the polar bear capital of the world.

我们住在世界北极熊之都外面的一个研究营地。

The goal was to promote the equal use of all six tongues.

目标是促进六种语言的平等使用。

7 像某类人或物的特征

Of 指某人具有另一类人或事物的那种特性或特征，常见于书面表达。

Every one of my volunteers is a man filled with the courage of a lion.

我的每一位志愿者都是充满狮子般勇气的人。

His eyes were the eyes of a drunkard and a fanatic.

他的双眼里显出醉酒者和狂热之徒的神情。

8 关于 / 涉及的内容

Of 用来指与某事物有关或该事物所涉及的内容。

What Hammond is referring to is the question of meaning.

哈蒙德所指的是意义的问题。

The world will have a better understanding of China and its rich history.

世界将更好地了解中国及其丰富的历史。

9 动词搭配

Of 与一些动词连用，指与行为有关的事，尤其涉及认识、交流、态度或去除等意义。

Remind your child of their camp account so they can purchase items from the camp store if necessary.

提醒你的孩子他们的营地账户，这样必要时他们可以在营地商店购买物品。

Think of a rubber band.

想象一根橡皮筋。

10 形容词搭配

Of 与某些形容词连用，指与感觉或特性有关的事。

Every language is worthy of respect and should be treated equally.

每一种语言都值得尊重，也应该被平等对待。

The class was full of lovely people who glided effortlessly.

这个班里满是可爱的人，他们轻松地滑行着。

11 动作名词的对象或执行者

Of 与表示行为的名词连用，说明人或物受某种行为影响，或执行某一行动。

DM, a company focused on the development of AGI, has an ambition to solve intelligence.

DM 是一家专注于通用人工智能发展的公司，它有解决智能问题的雄心。

The use of those plastics has increased by 300% since 2019.

自 2019 年以来，这些塑料的使用增加了 300%。

12 经历到的事物或感觉

Of 可用来详细说明出现过或人们经历过的事物。

By 9:33 a.m., a burst of additional traffic might push it to 145 seconds.

到上午 9 点 33 分，一波额外车流可能会把时间推到 145 秒。

We acquire what we call a sense of right and wrong.

我们获得了所谓的是非感。

13 施动者 / 支持者 / 所有者

Of 表示所提及的人所做的事、受那人影响的东西，或他对某事持有的态度。

Supporters of self-driving cars say that speed limits could be raised.

自动驾驶汽车的支持者说，限速可以提高。

Lynn Lewis, director of Notting Hill Pathways, said they were happy to be taking part in the project.

诺丁山路径项目主任林恩·刘易斯说，他们很高兴参与这个项目。

14 具有某种性质

Of 用来指某人或某物具有的特点或特性。

Two qualities make the difference between men of great achievement and men of average performance.

有两种品质区分了成就卓越的人和平庸表现的人。

The far side of the moon is of particular interest to scientists.

月球背面让科学家尤其感兴趣。

15 年龄 / 数量

Of 表示年龄或数量。

For the study, 191 women with an average age of 50 took a bicycle exercise test.

在这项研究中，191 名平均年龄为 50 岁的女性进行了自行车运动测试。

Read our Youth Policy Guidelines for youth under the age of 15.

请阅读我们面向 15 岁以下青少年的青年政策指南。

16 材料 / 构成

Of 引出构成某事物的原材料。

The design features ten steel sepals made of glass and aluminium.

这一设计包含十片由玻璃和铝制成的钢制“萼片”。

Genius and IQ are composed of different mental constructs.

天才和智商由不同的心理结构构成。

17 情感引起的反应

Of 与 gasp、cry 等名词连用，表示导致某种反应的情感。

Jeff's hand touched the hot burner and he gave a cry of pain.

杰夫的手碰到了滚烫的炉头，他疼得叫了一声。

The man froze for a second, but when he saw the skinny boy he breathed a sigh of relief.

那人愣了一秒，但看到那个瘦小的男孩时，他松了一口气。

18 准确说明某事物

Of 还可用来准确地表示某事物，常跟 -ing 分词结构。

It most often centers on applying technical advancements to simplify the process of obtaining a product or service.

它最常围绕应用技术进步来简化获得产品或服务的过程。

These writers never get in the habit of crossing out chunks of their draft.

这些作者从来没有养成划掉草稿大块内容的习惯。

19 某时期的事件

Of 在某日期或某一段时间前，表示所叙述的事情发生的时间。

Since the early days of AI, imagination has outpaced what is possible or even probable.

自人工智能早期以来，想象力一直跑在可能性甚至概率的前面。

He lost his seat in the election of 1974.

他在 1974 年的选举中失去了席位。

20 事件发生的时间

Of 用在表示事件发生的时间名词后，说明那是什么事件。

On the day of the accident, Mrs. Brown was at work and Eric was at home with his father.

事故发生那天，布朗太太在上班，埃里克和父亲在家。

Christmas is a great time of year for children to communicate with others through writing.

圣诞节是一年中孩子们通过写作与他人交流的好时机。

21 死于某种原因

Of 可用来说明导致某人死亡的疾病、伤害或原因。

I met a modern-day Juliet who died of heartbreak after her parents rejected the love of her young life.

我遇到过一个现代版朱丽叶，她的父母拒绝了她年轻生命中的爱情后，她心碎而死。

The hospital said Miss Garbo died of heart failure.

医院说嘉宝小姐死于心力衰竭。

22 对某人行为的评价

Of 用在某动作施动者的名词前，表示说话者对所发生事件的看法。

I think it's very nice of him to take it on.

我觉得他愿意承担这件事，真是很好。

I'm sorry about this morning; it was stupid of me.

今早的事我很抱歉，是我太愚蠢了。

高频介词 as

as　12 个单项用法

1 身份 / 功能

As 指某人或某事物的身份、被认为具有的身份，或它们的功用。

As a main promoter of the International Tea Day, China has a responsibility to work with other countries.

作为国际茶日的主要推动者，中国有责任与其他国家合作。

I chose Paul Revere's horse as my subject.

我选择保罗·里维尔的马作为我的主题。

It serves as a reminder that there are many ways to address problems of sustainability.

它起到提醒作用：解决可持续性问题有很多方式。

2 动词 + as + 身份或评价

View、see、regard、describe、consider、know、use、serve 等动词后接 as，表示“把……看作 / 称为 / 用作……”。

Societies all over the world view the concept of an adult as self-supporting and self-directed.

世界各地的社会都把“成人”理解为能够自立、自主的人。

The young banker who eats lunch at his desk is probably seen as a go-getter.

那个在办公桌前吃午饭的年轻银行职员，很可能被看作有冲劲的人。

Chinese cultural elements commemorating Tang Xianzu, who is known as the Shakespeare of Asia, add an international character to Stratford-upon-Avon.

纪念汤显祖的中国文化元素，为莎士比亚故乡埃文河畔斯特拉特福增添了国际色彩；汤显祖被称为“亚洲的莎士比亚”。

3 人生阶段

As a child / as a teenager 表示在孩提时、青少年时期或某一身份阶段。

As a little girl, I wished to be a zookeeper when I grew up.

小时候，我希望长大后成为动物园管理员。

I experienced years of loneliness as a child.

小时候，我经历了多年的孤独。

4 同级比较

As...as 表示两者在程度上相同或接近；not as...as 表示不如……。

The heart just isn't as efficient in processing oxygen as it used to be.

心脏处理氧气的效率已经不如过去那样高了。

Basically, it means eating only as much food as your body needs.

基本上，它的意思是只吃身体需要的那么多食物。

5 举例

Such as 用来列举同类人或事物。

These plants included modern Western favourites such as rosemary, lavender and fennel.

这些植物包括现代西方人喜爱的迷迭香、薰衣草和茴香等。

Many of us turn to food to deal with unpleasant emotions such as sadness, loneliness, or boredom.

我们许多人会借食物来应对悲伤、孤独或无聊等不愉快情绪。

6 并列补充

As well as 表示“不但……而且…… / 以及”，把前后内容并列补充。

Silk as well as many plant species came to Britain for the first time.

丝绸以及许多植物物种首次传入英国。

The goal was to celebrate cultural diversity as well as to promote the equal use of all six tongues.

目标是庆祝文化多样性，并促进六种语言的平等使用。

7 时间同时

As 引导从句，表示两个动作或情况同时发生。

As Elli and I cooked dinner, a young male polar bear sniffed and smelled our garlic bread.

埃莉和我做晚饭时，一只年轻的雄性北极熊闻到了我们的蒜香面包。

A feeling of helplessness washed over me as I saw the bus queue thinning out.

当我看到公交车队伍越来越短时，一种无助感涌上心头。

8 随着变化

As 引导从句，表示随着某情况变化，另一情况也发生变化。

As one's IQ increases, there is an increased probability that they are a genius.

随着一个人的智商提高，他成为天才的可能性也会增加。

Yet as the months of reducing waste continued, I did what I could that was within my own reach.

然而，随着减废的几个月持续下去，我做了自己能力范围内能做的事。

9 原因 / 既然

As 引导从句，表示原因或背景，相当于“因为、既然”。

I didn't bother taking a look, as I had assumed that it would be stuffed full of cheesy love stories.

我懒得去看，因为我以为那里会塞满俗套的爱情故事。

Nobody responded to him as they were in the middle of trying to save a stranger's life.

没有人回应他，因为他们正在设法挽救一个陌生人的生命。

10 方式 / 按照

As 表示按照某种方式、约定或惯例来做事。

Four days later, when I was back in Vienna, I called Gunter as promised.

四天后，我回到维也纳，按约定给冈特打了电话。

The sign-up and registration process is as follows.

报名和注册流程如下。

Poppy welcomed them home as usual.

波比像往常一样欢迎他们回家。

11 结果

As a result 表示“结果、因此”。

As a result, there was a lot of leftover cheese brine.

结果，剩下了很多奶酪盐水。

Program evaluations show that kids eat more vegetables as a result of the classes.

项目评估显示，由于这些课程，孩子们吃了更多蔬菜。

12 话题转换

As for 或 as to 引出与前面内容有关的新主题，表示“至于、关于”。

As for Bailey, she is more puzzled about why her act is considered a big deal.

至于贝利，她更困惑的是为什么她的举动会被认为是大事。

The tenant has no information as to their address.

房客没有关于他们地址的信息。

同一个位置，可以说明主语

先找主语。 再看动词后面是不是在说明主语。 说明主语怎么样、是什么、保持什么状态、变成什么、结果证明是什么，常常就是表语。

be

It was sweet and fresh.

look sound feel smell taste

They never look lazy or untidy.

他们从不显得懒散或邋遢。

The man's words sounded pretty convincing.

那个男人的话听起来相当令人信服。

You should feel satisfied at the end of a meal, but not too full.

一顿饭结束时，你应该感到满足，但不要太饱。

The sun-dried clothes smell especially pleasant where I live.

French strawberries do not taste like cardboard.

法国草莓尝起来不像硬纸板。

seem appear

这所房子安静得像坟墓一样。

Some of your interests may not appear to be connected.

prove turn out

Finding a small neighborhood in a vast country proved to be impossible.

在一个辽阔的国家里找到一个小社区，结果证明是不可能的。

The birds have proved skilful at tool use while kept in the cage.

这些鸟在笼养时已经证明很擅长使用工具。

A bright spot for me turned out to be reading.

对我来说，一个亮点原来是阅读。

We all want to hope things will turn out fine.

我们都希望事情最终会好起来。

保持简单。

艺术家和摄影师继续被这个特殊的地方吸引。
