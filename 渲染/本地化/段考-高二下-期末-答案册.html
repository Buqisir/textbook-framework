练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. becoming表结果，用 -⁠ing
  2. Compared逻辑主语 online education 与 compare 是被动关系，用过去分词 Compared 作状语。
  3. pacingpace 主动，伴随状语
  4. Having acquiredengineers 主动，用完成式
  5. Annoyed by the stranger's rude manners, the waitress refused to serve him.waitress 被打扰，用 -⁠ed
  6. to findonly to find 表结果
  7. look after照料，表目的
  8. to be admittedthe first 后不定式作定语
  9. livingpeople 生活，用现在分词
  10. directedfilm 被导演，用过去分词
  11. to have brokenproud to have done
  12. to have experiencedseem to have done
  13. team up withhow to 作宾语
  14. measuredhave sth done
  15. accusedhear sb. done，宾语与动作是被动关系
  16. With the design completed, he conducted several tests to see whether it was practical.design 被完成，用过去分词
  17. to acquirefor sb. to do 句型

专题 2　时态与被动

  1. have been starving / was starving持续状态，现在完成进行时或过去进行时
  2. dragged并列谓语 had 为过去式，故用过去式
  3. was invited一般过去时的被动，谓语随 Tom
  4. Effective measures have been taken to protect the endangered animals by the government.现在完成时被动，measures 为复数
  5. By the end of last week, the book had been translated into three languages (by her).过去完成时被动，had been done
  6. Many explorers have been inspired by the hero’s tales to search for sea routes.现在完成时被动，宾语作主语
  7. The new system is being tested out by them to see if it works well.现在进行时被动，is being done
  8. to be rewardedbe supposed to do，you 与 reward 是被动关系
  9. being leftmind doing，child 与 leave 是被动关系

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. itconsider it adj. 句型
  2. itfind it adj. to do
  3. howIt 作形式主语，how 从句为主语

专题 4　定语从句

  1. where先行词是 platform；用 where
  2. why / is thatreason 之后用 why
  3. which指 the design，非限制
  4. He has made great contributions to environmental protection, which deserves our respect.which 指前句，非限制
  5. that指人或物，限定性
  6. whomsome of whom 指人

专题 5　名词性从句

  1. What the story relays/conveys to us is that perseverance can prevent us from abandoning/giving up our dreams easily.that 只起连接作用
  2. What most people are concerned about is whether we can have a better future.What 引导主语从句
  3. Whether引导主语从句
  4. What作主语，缺主语
  5. thatword 之后的同位语从句
  6. whether she could / if she couldwhether 或 if 引导宾语从句，主语 I 改为 she，can 改为 could

专题 6　倒装

  1. did the volunteers organizenot only 句首，did 提前
  2. did / adjustOnly after 句首部分倒装，did 后用原形
  3. Not until / didnot until 句首，did 提前

专题 7　状语从句

  1. before时间状语，before 引导
  2. unless条件状语，unless 表示除非
  3. where地点状语，where 引导
