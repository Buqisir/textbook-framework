  7. to defend表目的，不定式作状语

  9. Not knowingnot 放分词前，作原因状语
  10. exposing洪水冲刷，主动用现在分词
  11. Not toleratinghe 不能忍受噪声，主动用现在分词

  16. wrappingspend 时间 doing

专题 2　名词性从句

  1. It seemed thatIt 作形式主语，that 为真正主语
  2. What impresses me most is that he is always committed to helping others.What 作主语，that 引导表语从句
  3. What缺主语，作主语从句
  4. What, astonishedWhat 作主语，astonished 作宾补
  5. what作表语，指以前的样子
  6. The reason why scientists attach importance to environmental protection is that the environment is crucial to human survival.表示原因的表语从句
  7. What made the Sports Meet successful was that every class had been training hard in preparation for the big event.What 作主语，that 引导表语从句
  8. when引导表语从句，表示时间
  9. whether介词后用 whether 表是否
  10. howtell how 表示「多么」

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. itconsider it 作形式宾语
  2. itthought 后用 it 作形式宾语
  3. it her dutyit 形式宾语，her duty 作宾补
  4. It was thought thatthought that 被动句型
  5. is likely to be句型转换
  6. It doesn't matter to meIt doesn’t matter 句型

专题 4　定语从句

  1. which非限制性，which 作 was 的主语
  2. who is so absorbed非限制性，who 指人作主语
  3. when先行词是时间，用关系副词 when
  4. where先行词是地点，用关系副词 where

专题 5　状语从句

  1. if necessaryif 从句省略 it is
  2. unless相当于 if……not
  3. exposedif 从句省略 be，留 exposed
  4. When left alone in the dark last night, Tom felt frightened and helpless.从句省略 he was，留 left
  5. Hardly had the girl seen the doctor when she cried.hardly 置于句首，所在分句部分倒装，用 had the girl seen
  6. Though / Although / While三者均可表示让步
  7. while表对比
  8. such ... thatso……that 改为 such
  9. so ... thatsuch 改为 so 加形容词

专题 6　倒装

  1. No sooner had I got home than it began to rain.No sooner……than 倒装
  2. Not until I was chosen monitor of the class did I realize the importance of teamwork.Not until 倒装 did I
