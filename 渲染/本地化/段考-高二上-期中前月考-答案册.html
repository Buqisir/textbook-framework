专题 1　非谓语

  1. to predictability 后接不定式作定语
  2. to be held将来被动，用 to be done
  3. So far, more than 500 students have signed up for the marathon to be held next month.将来被动，用 to be done
  4. exposedpeople 被动，用过去分词
  5. to look不定式作后置定语，表示有待完成的动作
  6. consisting主动关系，用现在分词作后置定语
  7. stretchingwith 复合结构，海水主动延伸
  8. Looking out of the window, she found a cat on the grass.she 看向窗外，主动用现在分词
  9. You’d better combine exercise with a balanced diet to keep healthy.表目的，用不定式作状语
  10. Walking along the street, he sang a popular song.he 走在路上，主动用现在分词
  11. recalling与主语 he 主动，表示伴随
  12. shiftingJoe 移动脚步，主动表示伴随
  13. helping大学帮助人，主动用现在分词
  14. leaving主动表结果，用现在分词
  15. consistingconsist 不用于被动，用现在分词
  16. completed省略 it is，保留过去分词
  17. kept in touch withkeep in touch with 为固定搭配
  18. keep/stay in touchkeep 与 stay 均可，与 in touch 搭配
  19. in charge ofbe in charge of doing 表示负责做某事
  20. keeping his promise/wordof 后接动名词，someone 是其逻辑主语
  21. to blamebe to blame for 表示应对……负责
  22. to blamebe to blame 为固定搭配
  23. analyzetry to 后接动词原形
  24. to arrangemanage to 表示设法做成
  25. Being exposed to被动关系的动名词短语作主语
  26. Attending this event will help you understand how to be optimistic.Attending 作主语，谓语用单数
  27. exposedmake 后宾补用 done
  28. heardmake 后宾补用 done

专题 2　名词性从句

  1. It is apparent形式主语 It，that 作主语
  2. What, surprisedWhat 作主语，surprised 作宾补
  3. What delights usWhat 从句作主语，谓语用单数
  4. It is apparent that the two statements were contradictory in many ways.形式主语 It，that 为主语
  5. what主语从句中 what 作 in 的宾语
  6. What impressed me most is that students have a strong passion for English in the event held last week.What 作主语，that 引导表语从句
  7. what在从句中作主语
  8. whythat is why 表示原因
  9. We should be grateful for what our parents give us. / We should appreciate what our parents give us.for 后接 what 引导的宾语从句
  10. what在从句中作 mean 的宾语
  11. thatfact 后的同位语从句
  12. thatsign 后的同位语从句

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. Itthat 从句作主语，用 it
  2. It作形式主语，that 从句作主语
  3. Itseem 前用 It，that 后置
  4. It seemed that nobody knew anything about the matter.改为 It seemed that 句型
  5. itmake it 为固定搭配
  6. itthink 后用 it 作形式宾语

专题 4　状语从句

  1. the moment/instant/secondthe moment 表示一……就
  2. wateringwhen 从句省略为现在分词
  3. No matter whowhoever 即无论谁
  4. exposedif 从句省略，保留 exposed
  5. such an interestingso……that 改为 such 句型

专题 5　定语从句

  1. whichnone of which 表示「其中没有一个」
  2. which非限制性，先行词是整个句子
  3. where指地点，关系副词 where 作状语
  4. when指时间，关系副词 when 作状语

专题 6　倒装

  1. Not until the war was over were they allowed to return to their hometown.not until 句首，主句部分倒装
  2. did I realizeonly 状语句首，主句倒装
