段考专题　高二上　答案册

期中后月考

练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. submittedproposals 是被提交的，用过去分词
  2. The Museum of Art built/completed/constructed last year is worthy of being visited.built 修饰 museum
  3. stuffedmooncakes 被填充，用过去分词
  4. Having spent与主语主动关系，动作在先，用完成式
  5. Encouraged by my grandfather, I signed up for the competition.I 被鼓励，用过去分词
  6. to strengthenbe designed to 表示目的
  7. how to start疑问词加不定式作宾语
  8. It is essentialIt 作形式主语，that 为真正主语
  9. Strengthening动名词短语作主语，谓语用单数
  10. stolenhave sth stolen 表遭遇

专题 2　it 形式主宾语

  1. It is wise of him to decide to restore the vase to its original beauty.wise of sb. to do 句型
  2. itconsider 后用 it 作形式宾语

专题 3　名词性从句

  1. that系动词 was 后；that 引导说明性内容
  2. What impressed me most was that she was very optimistic about the future.What 作主语，that 引导表语从句
  3. Whether引导主语从句，表示是否

专题 4　虚拟语气

  1. should be cut / be cut建议戒除甜饮料，用 (should) be cut
