练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. On the eve of Chinese New Year, all the families gather around, having a big dinner.having a big dinner 作伴随状语
  2. Seeing his amazing performance, his parents were moved to tears.Seeing 表示主动，parents 看到表演
  3. so as to表示目的
  4. Feeling uncomfortable, Luke stopped to have a rest.Feeling 表示主动，Luke 感到不适
  5. to keep in touch表示目的
  6. helping表示原因，people 是主动
  7. adapted from作定语，film 被改编
  8. committing作定语，those 是主动
  9. inferringadvocate 后接动名词
  10. found … misunderstoodfind oneself 后接过去分词作宾补
  11. is beneficial to动名词短语作主语，谓语用单数
  12. Making动名词短语作主语，谓语随之变化
  13. was rejected被动语态
  14. Having been simplifiedHaving been done，完成式被动分词

专题 2　it 形式主宾语

  1. It is vital that everyone should make contributions to the society.It is vital that 句型，that 从句作真主语
  2. It was his duty to attend to this matter.It was his duty to 句型，to 短语作真主语
  3. It is acknowledgedthat 句型
  4. itmakes 后；it 指代不定式短语
  5. it作形式宾语，that 从句是真宾语

专题 3　定语从句

  1. Our English teacher is humorous, which makes our class filled with joy.which 指代前面整句，用非限制从句
  2. As引导非限制从句，指代整个句子
  3. wherestage 后 where 表示阶段或情况

专题 4　状语从句

  1. When asked about his address, the little boy didn’t respond.When asked 省略 he was，表示被问到
  2. immediately表示时间上的紧接
  3. the instant引导，表示一……就
  4. If necessary是固定用法

专题 5　强调句

  1. It is … that强调时间状语
  2. It was not until all the fish in the river died out that they realized the importance of protecting the environment.It was not until … that 强调时间

专题 6　主谓一致

  1. benefits动名词短语作主语，谓语用单数
  2. wasrather than 不计入主语，谓语随 driver
