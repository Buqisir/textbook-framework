段考专题　高一上　答案册

期中后月考

练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. survivechance 后接不定式作定语
  2. identifysomething 后接不定式作定语
  3. keep me companykeep sb. company 陪伴某人
  4. managedmanage to do，不定式作宾语
  5. is of benefit动名词主语，谓语用单数

专题 2　定语从句

  1. whichby 之后指物用 which
  2. who were trapped由过去分词 trapped 改写为定语从句
  3. whommost of 之后指人用 whom

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. it / to getfind it difficult to do sth.
  2. It is reported据报道的固定句型
  3. itappreciate it if 固定句型

专题 4　虚拟语气

  1. (should) be establishedrecommendation 的同位语从句
  2. (should) be returnedbooks 是承受者，用被动

专题 5　倒装

  1. Only when we concentrate on our studies will/can we make progress and finally enter the university (that/which) we dream of.only when 从句，主句 will 提前
  2. Only by studying hard can you make great progress in English./Only when you work hard can you make great progress in English.only 句首，助动词 can 提前
