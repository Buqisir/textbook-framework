练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. to exploremake efforts 后接不定式
  2. To launch句首不定式表示目的
  3. Knowing that they would face various situations, the astronauts trained hard for the mission.分词作状语，与主语 astronauts 主动
  4. indicating逗号后 -⁠ing 表示结果
  5. Deeply inspired by分词作状语，he 是被动受启发
  6. releasing表结果，与主语 she 主动
  7. Having lost完成式分词，先于谓语动作
  8. workingheroes 与 work 是主动关系
  9. writtenword 与 write 是被动关系
  10. originally celebrated过去分词作后置定语，表被动
  11. to travel intofirst 修饰名词，后接不定式
  12. to getchance 之后接不定式作定语
  13. to be conducted不定式作后置定语，表示待完成的动作
  14. children running宾补用现在分词；宾语主动执行动作
  15. comingkeep 后宾语执行动作，用 doing
  16. regularly副词修饰动名词 reading
  17. Nothing is more inspiring than seeing our country grow stronger and stronger/become more and more powerful.Nothing 作主语，seeing … 动名词短语作 than 后的比较对象
  18. make the most of / make full use oftend to 后接动词原形，充分利用
  19. insisted on … givinginsist on doing 坚持做某事
  20. has been gathered句中唯一的谓语，用现在完成时被动

专题 2　定语从句

  1. where先行词 stage 表地点，用 where
  2. when先行词 days 表时间，用 when
  3. who先行词是人，作主语用 who
  4. where逗号后的非限制性从句，地点用 where

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. to selectit 作形式宾语，a duty 作宾补，to select … 是真正的宾语
  2. bear it in mind / keep it in mindbear it in mind 牢记，it 指代后文
  3. itbelieve it adj. to do 结构
  4. It is surprising that / It surprises us thatIt 作形式主语，that 引导后置的主语从句，说明令人惊讶的事情。

专题 4　名词性从句

  1. What impressed me on my last trip was the local customs.What 引导主语从句

专题 5　状语从句

  1. Thinking of主语相同，分词作原因状语
  2. In spite of相当于 despite
  3. If真实条件用 if 引导，主句用将来时
  4. Just as Tom was about to give up, his teacher extended a helping hand to him.just as 表示正当……时
  5. in order to / so as to目的，后接动词原形

专题 6　倒装

  1. can he produceonly 置于句首，主句倒装
  2. did he attachonly 置于句首，主句倒装
  3. Not only can sports keep us healthy, but they also help us make friends.Not only 位于句首，主句倒装

专题 7　强调句

  1. It was / thatIt was … that 强调主语 Tom’s knowledge
  2. that强调主语，强调句中不用 which
