专题 2　it 形式主宾语

  1. Many freshmen find it quite challenging to make a workable schedule for both studies and extra-curricular activities.find it + adj. + to do 改写
  2. itthink it + adj. + to do
  3. itfind it + adj. + to do
  4. You will find it helpful to join in some extra-curricular activities.that 从句改为 find it + 形容词 + to do。
  5. It is obviousIt is adj. that 句型，that 题干已给出。
  6. itfind it + 形容词 + to do。
  7. It is obvious thatIt is adj. that 句型
  8. Itis obvious that 句型，it 作形式主语。
  9. itmake it + 形容词 + for sb. to do。
  10. It is our responsibility to protect the environment.It is 名词 to do：不定式作真正主语。

专题 3　虚拟语气

  1. It is recommended thatIt be + p.p. + that；原句一般现在时，用 is
  2. (should) be checkedmachines 被检查，用 be checked。
  3. that you should focus onrecommend 后从句用 should 原形
  4. (should) be boughtdictionary 是 buy 的承受者，用被动
  5. What if改写成 What if，从句时态不变
  6. concentrate/focus your energy onrecommend 后从句用 should 原形
  7. Mrs Brown recommended that I (should) take part in after-class activities actively.recommend 后从句用 (should) + 动词原形。
  8. The teacher recommended that we (should) not be addicted to playing computer games.recommend 后宾语从句用 should 原形
  9. The teacher recommends that we (should) spend an hour every day on English study.recommend 后用 (should) 原形；须译“每天”

专题 4　名词性从句

  1. which修饰 courses，引导宾语从句。
  2. howwonder 后的宾语从句缺状语，用 how
  3. thatIt 作形式主语，that 引导后置的主语从句，说明显而易见的事情。
  4. It is obvious that用 It is obvious that 改写，it 作形式主语，that 引导后置的主语从句。

专题 5　强调句

  1. It is when I meet with difficulties that my teacher gives me encouragement.被强调的是 when 从句，结构不变。
  2. It is senior high school that is a challenge.强调主语，用 that
  3. It was with the encouragement of his family that he successfully started his own IT company.状语强调只用 that，不用 who。
  4. It was his adventurous/thrill-seeking personality that inspired/motivated/encouraged his friends to try new things/try something new.强调主语，that 连接。
