专题 1　非谓语

  1. ranking现在分词主动，表结果
  2. Releasedchemical 被释放，用过去分词
  3. Absorbed in作原因状语
  4. Appointedhe 被任命，用过去分词
  5. abandoningthey 主动，表伴随用 -⁠ing
  6. Inspired by the heroic story, the young man decided to realize his dream of being a famous doctor.man 被激励，用过去分词
  7. to findonly to do 表意外结果
  8. to help省略 do，to help 表目的
  9. originating修饰 form，主动用 -⁠ing
  10. locatedcity 位于，用过去分词
  11. The manager received a call from an applicant asking for further information.applicant 主动，用 -⁠ing
  12. come to life / coming to life感官动词后用原形或 doing 作宾补
  13. stressed outfeel 为系动词，stressed out 作表语，非宾补
  14. to writeassign sb. to do
  15. pollutingwater 主动，用 -⁠ing
  16. to be admittedambition 后接被动不定式
  17. to developpurpose 后接不定式作表语
  18. to raiseaim 后接不定式表目的
  19. to enhancebe thought to do
  20. to be respecteddeserve 后接被动不定式
  21. to be lyingseem to be doing 型
  22. studyingspend 时间 doing；固定搭配
  23. worthy of being readworthy of being done
  24. To ensure that the project is completed on time is my responsibility.不定式短语作主语，谓语单数

专题 2　定语从句

  1. where情况先行词用 where
  2. where情况先行词用 where
  3. I will never forget those days, when I was brought up in the countryside.表示时间，用 when
  4. that主语是 books，用 that
  5. There is no doubt that those who have great passion for life are more likely to succeed.先行词是人，those who
  6. who非限制性，指人用 who
  7. which非限制性，指前句用 which
  8. whichdepend on 中 on 提前；用 on which

专题 3　时态与被动

  1. to be watchedworthy 后接被动不定式
  2. was abandoned被动，一般过去时
  3. has been undergoingsince 1997，动作仍将持续
  4. have been doingfor 表时段，动作仍在进行
  5. Over the past decade, the government has been supporting the development of new energy vehicles.过去十年持续，现在完成进行
  6. Several environmental protection measures have been carried out by the local government to reduce air pollution over the past three years.现在完成被动，measures 为复数
  7. The problem of food safety in this area is being examined by the experts.现在进行被动，主语单数
  8. A big change is being brought to the artificial intelligence technology by Sora.bring sth. to sth.，sth. 作被动句主语
  9. When I came here at this time last year, the conference hall was being built by them.过去进行被动，用 was being built

  2. It is our responsibility to seize every opportunity to educate everyone about global warming.It is 名词 to do 句型
  3. It is impolite to make fun of people with physical disabilities.It is impolite to do
  4. It is a privilege for me to give a speech on behalf of our school.形式主语句，to do 为真主语
  5. to have leftIt is said that 改写
  6. itconsider it adj. 句型
  7. itmake it a rule to do
  8. itthink it adj. 句型
  9. quarrelingno use doing 作真宾语
  10. itappreciate it if 从句

专题 5　名词性从句

  1. There is no doubt that reading books can broaden our horizons and enrich our knowledge.no doubt that 从句
  2. thatthe idea that 从句
  3. Do you agree with the idea that students have classes on weekends?the idea that 从句
  4. is worthy ofworthy of 型，主语从句
  5. What makes me interested is that kangaroos cannot walk backwards, but move forwards.What makes me 作主语
  6. what作 makes 的主语
  7. Alice asked me if/whether I would go to the bookstore with her that evening.asked me if 从句
  8. What excites the students is that they will visit an art exhibition next week.What excites 作主语
  9. whether引导表语从句
