专题 1　非谓语

  1. SurroundedDongguan 被环绕，用过去分词
  2. causing作结果状语，表示主动
  3. Given more time, I would do the job much better.given 表示条件，改自 if 从句
  4. Absorbed in the wonderful performance, Tom didn’t hear his phone ringing.absorbed 作状语，Tom 被吸引
  5. Inspired by the speech, the young couple decided to take up the challenge.inspired by 表示原因，couple 被激励
  6. Feelingfeel 是主动，表示正在感受
  7. With no one to accompany her, she began chatting online to pass the time.with 复合，后接不定式作定语
  8. To publish表示目的
  9. Hearing the good news of the Chinese team, the boy jumped out of the bed.hearing 表示主动，boy 听到消息
  10. enabling作结果状语，表示主动
  11. rangingfrom … 修饰 events
  12. featuringfeature 与 movie 是主动，用 featuring
  13. ranging from … to …作后置定语
  14. absorbed逗号后分词，student 是被吸引
  15. surrounding表示主动，trees 环绕
  16. to travel into spacefirst 修饰 astronaut，后接不定式 to travel 作定语，说明进入太空这一经历。
  17. to understandenable sb. to do，宾补用不定式
  18. informedkeep sb. informed，宾补用过去分词
  19. brokenfind 后 window 被打破，用 broken
  20. keep her companydecide to 后接不定式，keep company
  21. to winfail to do，fail 后接不定式
  22. ignorehard to ignore，to 后接动词原形
  23. sought their fortuneseek one’s fortune 是固定搭配
  24. chattingpermit 后接动名词
  25. What worries me most is her staying up too late every night.that she stays … 改为 her staying … 作表语，What 主语从句不变
  26. It is a great honor for me to stand here and make a speech.形式主语 It is … to do 句型
  27. were announced被动语态
  28. Located in Dongguan city, Guanyin Mountain Forest Park features the unique and beautiful scenerybe located in 表示“位于”，改写时用过去分词 located 作状语。
  29. Absorbed作状语，teenagers 被吸引
  30. With the work finished, he went home happily.译文用 with 复合，work 被完成
  31. Inspiredinspire 是被动，用 Inspired
  32. concentratedwith 复合，mind 被集中，用 -⁠ed
  33. flowingwith 复合，stream 与 flow 是主动
  34. To earna living 表示目的
  35. pursueto pursue 表示目的

专题 2　定语从句

  1. where / whenoccasion 可看作场合或时刻，where、when 均可
  2. whenoccasions 指时刻，用 when
  3. whentime 后用 when 指时间
  4. whereatmosphere 后 where 表示在其中
  5. wherepoint 后 where 表示抽象的地点
  6. first celebrated非限制分词，which was 可省略
  7. is surroundedwhich is surrounded，用被动语态
  8. which指 Disneyland，作主语
  9. which指代前面的整个句子
  10. The man talking to our teacher is my father.who is 可简化为现在分词
  11. that在从句中作 fond of 的宾语
  12. Asis reported 指代整个主句

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. I think it very important to try to solve problems independently in our daily life.think 后 it 作形式宾语，adj. 作宾补
  2. it作形式宾语，that 从句是真宾语
  3. it作形式宾语，for Mike to move forward 不定式结构是真正的宾语
  4. it作形式宾语，to spend 短语是真宾语
  5. it difficult to completefound it adj. to do 的同义句
  6. It is important to keep a balance between study and rest.It is important to do 句型翻译

专题 4　状语从句

  1. Deeply attracted byAs 从句改分词，表示原因
  2. Now that表示既然，引导原因
  3. keep your eyes open留意，条件句
  4. will be harmed主句用将来时被动，if 从句现在时
  5. In spite of后接名词，表示尽管
  6. In spite of后接名词，表示尽管
  7. Though treatedThough 从句省略主语和 be
  8. He made such rapid progress in English that the teacher praised him.such … that 引导结果状语

专题 5　名词性从句

  1. What sets Qian Xuesen apart from most engineers is his constant desire to innovate and experiment.What 引导主语从句，表示“……之处”
  2. That引导主语从句，不作成分
  3. Please keep in mind that the more languages you understand, the more chances you will get.keep in mind 后接 that 宾语从句
  4. street crowdedfound 后宾语加宾补 crowded
  5. that引导同位语从句，解释 fact
