段考专题　高二上　答案册

期末

练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. Laughed句首过去分词，与 he 是被动
  2. Having anticipated what would happen, he worked out a solution in advance.先发生的动作用 having done
  3. sending逗号后 -⁠ing 表示结果
  4. Informed of the result of the match, he felt disappointed.he 被告知，用过去分词
  5. advertisedjob 与 advertise 是被动关系
  6. infectedperson 与 infect 是被动关系
  7. permissionwithout 后接名词
  8. confirmrefuse 后接动词原形
  9. to raise不定式作表语，说明愿望的内容
  10. disturbed表示主语的感受，用形容词化的 -⁠ed
  11. told / not to be祈使句改间接引语，用 not to do
  12. to droponly to do 表示出人意料的结果

专题 2　名词性从句

  1. What surprised usWhat 引导主语从句
  2. What made his parents depressed was that he behaved badly in school.系动词 was 之后是表语从句
  3. What benefits me most is that I become more confident.What … is that …，that 引导表语从句
  4. if he could / whether he could一般疑问句改宾语从句，用 if 或 whether
  5. what / a placecalled 后缺宾语，用 what；填 a place 也通

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. is believed thatIt is believed that 句型
  2. itthink it adj. that 从句
  3. Sometimes parents find it difficult to communicate with their children.find it + adj. + to do

专题 4　定语从句

  1. where逗号后非限制性从句，地点用 where
  2. when先行词 time 表时间，用 when
  3. I am fond of reading poems, from which I can learn a lot of beautiful words.后一句说明 poems，用 from which 合并
  4. whose表所属，修饰 performance

专题 5　时态与被动

  1. The potatoes had been sliced (by him) before I could prevent him.过去完成时，用 had been sliced
  2. Last week we visited the museum where my uncle worked ten years ago.last week 指过去，用一般过去时
  3. made sense事情已发生，用一般过去时
  4. being bannedin spite of 后接动名词被动式

专题 6　强调句

  1. It is his spirit of devotion that sets him apart from other doctors.强调主语，It is … that 句型
  2. that强调原因状语，用 that

专题 7　状语从句

  1. had come与过去事实相反，从句用过去完成时
