专题 1　非谓语

  1. exploringspend + 时间 + doing 固定搭配
  2. to understandpretend to do sth. 假装做某事
  3. attracted表原因，与 he 是被动关系
  4. to transportbe expensive to do 结构
  5. Preventing / To prevent动名词与不定式都可作主语
  6. make contributions to与前面的 study 并列，用原形
  7. to bepretend to do 假装做某事
  8. attracted toattract 与 Fred 是被动关系
  9. belonging tobelong to 无被动，用 -⁠ing
  10. We will take part in the summer camp held by your school, which makes us very happy.held 与 camp 是被动，用 -⁠ed
  11. keep track of掌握最新消息
  12. yourself surrounded宾语与 surround 是被动关系
  13. When it comes to固定句型，to 为介词
  14. causingwhich 从句改为现在分词作状语
  15. volunteering介词 of 后接动名词
  16. Affectedaffect 与逻辑主语是被动关系
  17. told / to keep mytell sb. to do，ask 也可以

专题 2　定语从句

  1. whomto 之后指人用 whom
  2. that先行词为人，关系词作主语
  3. where从句缺地点状语
  4. whosenovels 属于 Liu Cixin
  5. whosetheme 属于 contest
  6. which非限制，指前面整句内容
  7. whomfrom 之后指人用 whom

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. itfind it exciting to do sth.
  2. We think it very effective to make use of these English learning styles and strategies.it 作形式宾语，is 去掉
  3. itmake it a rule that 结构
  4. adapt to / adjust toadapt to、adjust to 的 to 是介词，后接名词或 doing
  5. find it convenientfind it 加形容词加 for sb. to do
  6. itkeep sth. in mind 固定短语
  7. itfind 后接宾语和宾补，宾语用 it

专题 4　强调句

  1. It was this outdoor activity that enabled the classmates to experience the beauty of nature.强调主语，过去时用 was
  2. that强调句的 that 不能省
  3. ① It was；② that强调状语，原句为过去时
  4. It is great love and patience that being a nurse calls for.强调宾语，其余部分照原句

专题 5　倒装

  1. Only when we truly/really understand our own culture can we proudly share it with the world.only when 从句，主句 can 提前
  2. Not only does doing housework exercise the body but also develop a sense of responsibility.does 提到 doing housework 前
  3. Not only does exercise improve our health, but (also) it can reduce/ease our pressure./Not only does doing sports keep us healthy, but (also) it can relieve our stress.Not only 句首，助动词 does 提前
  4. will you beonly 条件从句句首，will 提前
