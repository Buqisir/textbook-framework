专题 4　强调句

  1. It was the flash cards that she used to teach the kids new words last class.强调宾语，用 that
  2. It is mastering enough English words that makes you an excellent English learner.强调主语，用 that
  3. It is with your help that I have made such great progress in my spoken/oral English.强调介词短语 with your help。
  4. that强调宾语 your country，指物用 that。
  5. It was because he was ill that he didn’t go to school yesterday.强调原因状语，用 It was…that
  6. It was in the corner of the park that I found this stray cat.强调地点状语，用 that
  7. It was not until the earth began to shake that the villagers realized the earthquake had happened.not until 强调句，that 后接原句
  8. not until / thatnot until…that 固定句型。
  9. It was not until he won the match that he made the team.not until 整体放入强调部分。
  10. It was when I was walking in the street that I recognized him in the crowd.when 从句强调，时态不变。
  11. It was because she sat too long in front of the computer that her eyes suffered.强调原因状语从句，用 It was…that。
  12. It was not until I finished this letter that she came back home.not until 强调句，that 后接原句
