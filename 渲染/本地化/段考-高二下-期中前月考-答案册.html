段考专题　高二下　答案册

期中前月考

练一练答案

专题 1　非谓语

  1. givenunless 从句省略 they are，用过去分词
  2. absorbedvisitors 被吸引，用 -⁠ed
  3. Tired from the day’s hard work, he fell asleep soon.形容词作原因状语，省略 being
  4. consisting of / composed ofconsist of 不用于被动，用现在分词 consisting of；be composed of 省略关系词和 be 后，用过去分词 composed of
  5. to be broadcast表将来的被动，用 to be done
  6. The most basic/fundamental need/requirement is the need to understand and be understood.to understand 作后置定语
  7. to knowurge to do 作定语
  8. To form the habit of reading in their free/spare time is beneficial for students.不定式作主语，放句首
  9. to be heating进行时用 to be doing
  10. to have been killed应为被动完成式，不用 to kill
  11. turn him downdecline 的同义短语 turn down，代词放在中间
  12. bringing / to lifecontribute to 后接动名词
  13. made up his mind固定搭配，mind 后接 to
  14. to showpurpose 后接不定式表目的
  15. to enhancepurpose 后接不定式作表语
  16. to takeplan 之后的不定式说明内容
  17. is easy to understand形容词加不定式作表语

专题 2　定语从句

  1. where / in which地点先行词用 where
  2. where情况先行词用 where
  3. whomof whom 指人，非限制
  4. in front of which介词后用 which，先行词是物
  5. which非限制性，指前面的名词
  6. The enterprise frequently poured polluted water into the Li River, which was widely criticized by environmentalists.合并句子，which 指前句内容
  7. This is an episode in my life that I will never forget.限定性从句，that 代替 which

专题 3　it 形式主宾语

  1. Itthat 从句作真主语，句首用 It
  2. It is a great honour for us to attend the meeting.It 作形式主语，不定式为真主语
  3. itmake it hard to do
  4. itconsider 后用 it 作形式宾语

专题 4　名词性从句

  1. What从句 miss 缺宾语，用 What
  2. What surprised her应为 surprised her，不用 made her
  3. When computers will be more intelligent than humans is still a question.when 引导主语从句，表示时间
  4. that表语从句，that 只起连接作用
  5. what从句作 from 宾语，what 作 called 宾语
  6. what原误写 which；what 作从句主语，整个从句作 along 的宾语

专题 5　虚拟语气

  1. should be submitted / be submitteddemand 后 that 从句，被动
  2. (should) be taught to swimurge 后虚拟，用被动

专题 6　with 复合结构

  1. spenttime 与 spend 是被动关系
  2. to deletewith 复合中不定式作补足语
