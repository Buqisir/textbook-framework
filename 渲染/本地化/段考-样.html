recommend 型

10 题里 9 题
recommend：必修一 U1

东莞这一站考虚拟，几乎只考一种：recommend 后接 that 从句，从句谓语用 (should) + 动词原形。题目常把它藏在两处：主语从句 It is recommended that …，或者要求把 recommend sb. to do 改写成从句。

recommend that sb. (should) do | It is recommended that sth. (should) be done

主语是物
→ (should) be done

从句主语若是动作的承受者，套被动：(should) be done。should 可省，答案两种都给分；但 be 不能丢。

主语从句
→ (should) do

It is highly recommended that the government (take) measures to protect the environment. 2023 · 东莞外国语 · 高一上月考

解析

句意：强烈建议政府采取措施保护环境。

成分：that 从句是真正的主语，谓语空着；主句是 It is recommended，从句用虚拟。

故填 (should) take。

machines 被检查
→ (should) be checked

It is strongly recommended that the machines (check) every year. 2024 · 东莞高中 · 高一上月考

解析

句意：强烈建议每年检查这些机器。

成分：同上用虚拟；machines 是 check 的承受者，用被动。

故填 (should) be checked。

见 recommend that，从句谓语写 (should) + 原形；主语是物再加 be done。改写题把 recommend sb. to do 换成 recommend that sb. (should) do。

专题 4　虚拟语气

  1. The expert recommended that some research (should) be done before carrying out the experiment.research 是承受者，用 (should) be done。
  2. (that) you (should) fix / focus onrecommend that 从句用虚拟。
  3. Mrs Brown recommended that I (should) take an active part in after-class activities.
  4. What ifWhat if … = What would happen if …
